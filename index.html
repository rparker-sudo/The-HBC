Why The HBC

Winning attitudes. Cohesive teams.

The overarching aim of The HBC is to cultivate not only winning attitudes and athletes, but also cohesive teams.

  • Experienced coaches who have played and coached at the high school, college, club and national team levels
  • Training grouped by ability so every player is challenged at the right level
  • A strong emphasis on fundamentals, confidence and sportsmanship
  • Programs for boys and girls of all ages and experience
