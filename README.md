# The HBC Volleyball Club website

Static website for The HBC Volleyball Club, a boys and girls volleyball club in Huntington Beach, CA.
Live at https://rparker-sudo.github.io/The-HBC/ (GitHub Pages, rebuilt on every push to this branch).

## Site structure (three wings)
A strip at the top of every page switches between the wings.

**Club wing** (root folder): black, white and silver, with pink touches for the girls program
- `index.html`: club home with the boys/girls program chooser
- `about.html`, `coaches.html` (all staff), `contact.html`
- Shared training for everyone: `clinics.html`, `private-training.html`, `summer-camp.html`

**Boys wing** (`boys/`): black, white and silver, no pink
- `boys/index.html`, `boys/teams.html` (teams & tryouts), `boys/coaches.html`

**Girls wing** (`girls/`): black and pink
- `girls/index.html`, `girls/teams.html` (teams & tryouts), `girls/coaches.html`

`teams.html` is kept only as a redirect for old links.

## Editing
Pages are generated by `tools/build.py`. Change the text there and run:

```sh
python3 tools/build.py
```

Styles live in `css/styles.css`. Each wing's colors come from the `.theme-club`, `.theme-boys` and `.theme-girls` blocks at the bottom of that file.
Behavior (mobile menu, contact form) lives in `js/main.js`. The contact form preselects the program from `?program=boys` or `?program=girls`.

## Branding
- `images/logo-boys.png`: boys logo (black & white)
- `images/logo-girls.png`: girls logo (black & pink)
- Colors: black `#0a0a0a`, white, boys silver `#c3c7cd`, girls pink `#fb82ab` (deeper `#c2185b` for pink text on white)
- The wave lines and shark fin echo the girls logo.

## To update
- Coach bios: replace the placeholder "Coach Name" cards in `tools/build.py`.
- Tryout and camp dates: update the notices on the wing teams pages and `summer-camp.html`.
- Photos: add images to `images/` and swap them in for the logo `.visual` blocks.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server 8000`.
