How It Works

The tryout process

Tryout dates and times are announced each season by age group. Make-up sessions are usually offered for players who can't attend their scheduled date.

Register

Contact the club to get the current tryout schedule for your age group and register your player.

Try Out

Come ready to compete. Coaches evaluate skills, athleticism, attitude and how players work with others.

Commit

Players who receive an offer complete the commitment form to secure a roster spot for the season.

Girls tryout schedule: dates for the upcoming season will be posted here. For the latest information, call Kim at (714) 305-9309 or email kimlucerohb@gmail.com.

Get Ready

Prepare for tryouts

The best way to get ready is to get touches on the ball.

  • Join a weekly clinic at your skill level
  • Work on specific skills with private training
  • Arrive early, bring water and knee pads, and compete on every rep
