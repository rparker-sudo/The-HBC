How Clinics Work

4-week sessions. Start any time.

Our weekly clinics run in 4-week sessions, and players can jump in whenever they're ready. There's no need to wait for a new season.

  • Tuesdays & Thursdays, 4:00 – 5:30 PM
  • Players are grouped by skill level
  • Experienced coaches on every court
  • Open to boys and girls, club players or not

Clinic days and times can change by season, so please confirm the current schedule with Kim before your first session.

Choose Your Court

Three levels of training

Our experienced coaches help players learn the basics, build confidence, strengthen fundamentals and fine-tune their skills.

Level 1

Beginner

Learn the basics of passing, setting, serving and hitting in a fun, encouraging setting. Perfect for new players.

Level 2

Intermediate

Build confidence and strengthen fundamentals with more reps, game situations and team play.

Level 3

Advanced

Fine-tune skills, sharpen technique and train at a higher tempo to prepare for club and high school play.
